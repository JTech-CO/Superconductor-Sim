    What the browser solver does not claim

    • No full TDGL PDE solve or microscopic Eliashberg solver.
    • No 3-D FEM/BEM Maxwell solution, demagnetization correction, or calibrated levitation force.
    • No sample-specific Jc surface unless the user replaces the phenomenological parameters.
    • No chemical diffusion/annealing kinetics; chemistry is kept as a future coupled process model.
    • NIST copper fit values are support-material properties, not superconducting-layer properties.
